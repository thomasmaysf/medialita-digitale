# General rules

These rules apply to **all modules** of the Digital License path.

## Adults present

- All facilitators and supporting adults stay for the **full length** of the module.
- Anyone who is not leading discussion sits **at the back** of the room and helps with supervision and support for the young people.

## Materials

- **Photocopies kept to a minimum**; each student has a personal **notebook** for notes and activities.
- At the start of each session (from module 2 onward): **recap of the previous session** at the board.

## Digital license

- At the end of each module, the **digital license stamp** is added to each student’s participation record.

## Behavior and safety

- Lab activities (hardware teardown, etc.) only under guidance from prepared adults.
- Respect break times and exit rules agreed with the school.
