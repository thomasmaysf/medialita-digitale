# Digital pact

The **Digital pact** is the agreement among families, young people, and the school on how to use smartphones, tablets, computers, and other connected devices thoughtfully.

## Goals

- Create protected times and spaces (meals, sleep, study, free play).
- Support dialogue between parents and children about digital choices.
- Align home rules with the educational climate of the school community.
