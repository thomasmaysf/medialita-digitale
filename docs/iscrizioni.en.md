## How to register

Email **[patentinodigitale@scuolasteiner-trento.it](mailto:patentinodigitale@scuolasteiner-trento.it)**.

In the reply we will ask for:

- the child’s first and last name;
- class / grade;
- date of birth;
- **both** parents’ email addresses;
- the reference parent’s phone number.

Registration becomes **final** with a **€30** payment on the day of the **first module** (17 October 2026).

## Criteria

- Priority for students in the grades indicated by the program and for families who commit to the [Digital pact](patto-digitale.md).
- If you are on the waiting list, the organizing group will contact you if a place opens.
