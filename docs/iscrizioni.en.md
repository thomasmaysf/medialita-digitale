# Registration

**Local** page for the Trento edition. Groups replicating the path elsewhere replace this with their own process (see [How to run this yourself](come-replicare.md)).

!!! info "Our edition (Trento 2026/27)"
    - **Venue, general times, collaborators:** [Calendar — Our edition](calendario.md#edizione-trento).
    - **Audience:** brochure **ages 12–15** (with parents); **limited places**; for information and registration: [patentinodigitale@scuolasteiner-trento.it](mailto:patentinodigitale@scuolasteiner-trento.it)
    - **Attendance:** each module earns a **stamp in the booklet** (*libretto*).
    - **Absences:** communicate in advance; missed modules can be made up in a **later edition** of the program.
    - **Digital license (brochure rules):** for **grade 8 / Terza media**, certificate for students who attend **all modules** with **parents present** at the final meeting; for **grade 7**, planned delivery in **May 2028** at the final meeting (with encouragement to take extra modules in between).
    - Priority for families who join the [Digital pact](patto-digitale.md) when the text is available.
