# Module 2 — Symbols, phone materials, and photography

**Date:** Saturday 14 November 2026 · **Status:** scheduled

**Theme:** how images and symbols carry emotions, what a smartphone is made of and at what cost, and how photography moved from film to digital files.

**Why it matters:** Linking art, geography, and technology to everyday devices helps young people read emoji and photos more thoughtfully and ask *where* materials come from and *who* pays for production.

## Session outline (program)

### Part A — Art history: symbols and emotions, from origins to emoji (9:00–10:20)

With **Camilla**: how cultures have represented ideas and feelings over time, up to today’s short visual language (including emoji).

### Part B — Materials inside a smartphone (10:40–12:00)

With **Consuelo**, **Elisa**, and **Mpaliza**: where raw materials come from, how long they last and how they are recycled, environmental and human impacts of mining, including exploited child labour in some supply chains.

### Part C — History of photography, from film to file (12:00–13:20)

With **Thomas** and **Stefano**: from slides and coloured gels to a lab with a webcam and apps that change colours and pixels — what shifts when an image becomes numbers.

### Part D — Parents’ evening (optional)

Evening session with **Consuelo**, **Elisa**, and **Mpaliza** on the module’s themes; **venue to be confirmed**.
