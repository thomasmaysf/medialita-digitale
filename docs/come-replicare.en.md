# How to run this yourself

This wiki is an **open-source playbook**: modules, labs, and discussion circles that other schools, associations, or parent groups can **copy, translate, and adapt**. The goal is not a closed course product, but sharing recipes tested in the room.

## Course philosophy

- **Young people and parents together** — not “kids’ class only”: adults join circles and labs where indicated, so conversation continues at home.
- **Hands and head** — each module combines a **circle** (Part A), **guided inquiry or theory** (Part B), and a **lab** (Part C); optionally **Part D** (e.g. a parents’ evening).
- **Family digital pact** — shared agreements on time, space, and devices ([Digital pact](patto-digitale.md) as a template to rewrite for your context).
- **License / certificate** — a simple document with a **stamp or mark per completed module**; it recognises participation, not a grade.

## Typical session format

| Block | Indicative time | Content |
|-------|-----------------|---------|
| Part A | 9:00–10:20 | Circle: welcome, questions, expectations |
| Break | 10:20–10:40 | |
| Part B | 10:40–12:00 | Theory, history, inquiry |
| Part C | 12:00–13:20 | Hands-on lab |
| Part D | optional | Evening or parents-only session |

Adapt times to your school calendar. Keep **two adults** (or more) present for labs with materials, teardown, or low-voltage electricity — see [General rules](regole-generali.md).

## Team roles

| Role | Tasks |
|------|--------|
| **Lead facilitator** | Holds the thread of the module, keeps time, includes everyone in the circle |
| **Support facilitator** | Supervision, handing out materials, helping smaller groups |
| **Topic expert** | Art history, geography/materials, photography, etc. — may lead a Part B or C |
| **Organising group** | Local calendar, registration, pact, communication with the school |

You do not need a full-time teacher: prepared volunteers who have read the module **before** the session.

## Adapting modules

1. **Age** — the 2026/27 Trento edition targets about **ages 12–15** with parents; for younger or older groups, shorten activities or always pair one adult with one young person.
2. **Time** — cut an optional activity rather than rushing Part C; the lab is what young people remember.
3. **Language and culture** — translate the `.en.md` files or write local variants; swap examples (emoji, maps) for your context.
4. **Local edition** — keep dates, names, and registration on a short page or in an “Our edition” box, not in the core recipe.

Modules **1** and **2** are written in depth; others still have **framing** only — you can expand them using the same pattern.
